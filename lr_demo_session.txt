● Yes. One warning first: my two render processes are using your 4 GB GPU for
  the next hour or so. Rendered mode in the viewport competes with them for
  VRAM, which can slow the render or crash Blender. Until the render finishes,
  Solid mode (Z → Solid) is safe for scrubbing cameras and timing.

  To make Rendered mode lighter
  1. Lower resolution: Render Properties → Performance → Viewport → Pixel Size →
     4x.
  2. Fewer samples: Render Properties → Sampling → Viewport Samples → 1–4.
  3. Cheaper shading: use Material Preview instead of Rendered (Z → Material
     Preview). Board text and colours still show.
  4. Fewer walkers: in the Outliner, hide the Crowd and Traffic collections (eye
     icon). The animated pedestrians are the heaviest part to play back.
  5. Smoother playback: in the Timeline, Playback → Sync → Frame Dropping. It
     plays at real speed and skips frames instead of slowing down.
  6. Smaller viewport: a smaller 3D view renders faster.
